# Reproduction scope

## In scope

- Official pretrained inference and verification on official examples
- Diverse custom inputs
- Camera prediction, depth-map, point-map, point-cloud, and exposed-confidence analysis
- Runtime and peak-memory measurement
- Effects of image count, image order, and first reference frame
- Qualitative strengths and failures
- Small quantitative experiments where ground truth is available
- Fine-tuning feasibility investigation

## Not currently in scope

- Full training from scratch
- Recreating the complete multi-dataset corpus or all benchmark tables
- Recreating 64-A100 training
- Claiming exact paper reproduction without evidence

No checkpoint, dataset, inference, benchmark, training, or fine-tuning was executed during initialization.
